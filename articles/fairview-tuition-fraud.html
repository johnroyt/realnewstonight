Fairview Woman Arrested for Posing as Native American to Save on Son's College Tuition

By Staff Reporter · Fairview

Fairview, May 22, 2024 — A Fairview woman has been arrested for falsely claiming Native American heritage in an effort to secure financial aid for her son's college education. The woman was taken into custody by Fairview police on Wednesday following an investigation that revealed her fraudulent claims.

According to authorities, she had applied for several thousand dollars in scholarships and tuition discounts reserved for Native American students despite being among the whitest people ever, even by Fairview standards. The investigation began when her impoverished sister, a member of Woodbridge Golf Club, alerted law enforcement.

"We're struggling to afford the dry cleaning bills on our son's crested private school shirts," the sister lamented from the golf course cafe. "Even though my husband's business recently expanded into the Cottage Cheese market, we're still struggling to make ends meet. To find out that my own sister is taking funds from other underserved children, I just couldn't stay silent any longer."

The woman allegedly provided forged documents and false statements to support her claim of being a member of a federally recognized tribe. Her son, who was unaware of the fraudulent scholarship, has not been charged in connection with the case.

"Sports, taxes, and inflation. They. Will. Get. You." She clapped at the reporter during questioning. "I did what I had to do."

Her father, when reached for comment, expressed confusion. "I don't know what she's talking about, I was born in Greece," he said. "Just look at my god-like physique."

The arrest has sparked a broader discussion about the verification processes for scholarships and financial aid programs. Many are calling for stricter measures to prevent similar cases in the future.

The woman faces charges of fraud and identity theft, and if convicted, she could face significant fines and prison time. Her attorney has declined to comment on the case.

This incident highlights the importance of integrity and honesty in the pursuit of education and financial assistance. The community remains watchful as the legal process unfolds, hoping for a resolution that upholds justice and fairness.
